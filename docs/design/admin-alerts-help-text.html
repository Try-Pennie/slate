Slate, /admin/alerts: alert switch help text

For whoever turns Slate's Slack alerts on and off on /admin/alerts. Settled over two design rounds and approved by Dan on 2026-10-26: round 1 drew his feedback that the page is too noisy and should show only the current state, and in round 2 he picked Trimmed; the same day he dropped the wait notes whose condition had been met. There is no PRD for this change, so the requirements below are his decisions, built on branch admin-alerts-below-tier-cleanup.

The settled design. Every frame is drawn inside the product's real chrome, captured from http://localhost:3100/admin/alerts, the production build served against the local e2e Supabase (seeded data, every switch off), signed in as the seeded admin ada.admin@slate-e2e.test through the real callback, 1440 wide, light theme, captured with capture-chrome.js in headless Chrome on 2026-10-26 at 12:57 PM ET, once the streamed content had replaced the loading skeleton, from the committed fixture the built page renders too. The build is done when it scores 100% against this file on both halves.

Requirements

  • 1 Every switch in the alerting card shows only the sentence for its current state, on or off, with no On: or Off: label and nothing that describes the form.
  • 2 Each sentence is the round 2 Trimmed text, kept true to what the monitor actually sends, and asks the reader to wait only where the reason still holds.
  • 3 The sentence follows the box as it is ticked or cleared, before the form is saved, and after a form reset.
  • 4 Slate's design rules carry the standing rule: a switch's help text says only what its current state means, in as few words as carry the facts.

Decisions

  1. D1 Each switch shows one sentence, the one for its box's state, never the other, with no On: or Off: label. Dan, 2026-10-26: the page is too noisy; show on OR off based on the current state. Requirement 1. Checked on f1, f2: for every switch in the fixture, its box is ticked exactly when the frame says, the text its aria-describedby names is that state's sentence, it holds nothing of the other state's sentence, and it carries no On: or Off: label.
  2. D2 No sentence says nobody is notified, and the card no longer says saving applies immediately with no deploy needed. The unticked box already says the first, and the second describes the form rather than the alert. Requirement 1. Checked on f1, f2: the alerting card's text holds neither 'nobody is notified', 'Saving applies' nor 'no deploy needed'.
  3. D3 The sentences read as drawn in f1 and f2, in the page's muted 12px help type under each switch. Round 2 pick, Trimmed. Requirement 2. Held by the picture half.
  4. D4 The below tier switch, when on, says one alert per call and another only on a change, and promises no message once a call is put right. It is an incident that posts once per call and again only on a change (#4235); a settled call has been silent since 2026-10-07. Requirement 2. Checked on f1: the below tier sentence, with its box ticked, says one alert per call and holds no 'follow up'.
  5. D5 The below tier switch, when off, names the cal.com cutover until cal.com is retired. Its reason to tick it changes at cutover; the retired wording is proven by the page test. Requirement 2. Checked on f2: the below tier sentence, with its box clear, says to turn it on before cutover.
  6. D6 Only bookings drop off asks the reader to wait while off; the other off sentences ask nobody to wait. Dan, 2026-10-26: advisors' hours are real, Regal is sending and watched, the pool has had its ordinary days, and real traffic reached the failure rate check on 2026-10-01; the pilot is held at 50% and the per page alarm judges only at 100%. Requirement 2. Checked on f1, f2: every clear box's sentence holds no 'leave this off', and bookings drop off's, when clear, says to turn it on once the traffic the floors were measured on reaches Slate.
  7. D7 The sentence follows the box live, before saving, and after a form reset. A saved state shown under a box that disagrees with it would contradict the control; the unsaved changes guard already marks the form. Requirement 3. Proven by src/app/admin/settings/page.test.tsx (swaps the sentence the moment a box is ticked or cleared; puts the sentence back with the box when the form is reset).
  8. D8 Slate's design rules state that a switch's help text says only what its current state means. Dan approved it as a standing rule on 2026-10-26. Requirement 4. Proven by .impeccable.md, Design Principles, item 4.

Exceptions Dan approved

None. A deliberate difference from this design is a new design round, or an exception Dan approves in his own words, recorded here.
