Coordinator booking page

The page where Pennie's scheduling coordinators book or move a customer's call from the Salesforce Lead (#4205). Settled over ten design rounds with Dan, ending 2026-10-26, drawn in the product's captured chrome with a coordinator's header.

The settled design. Every frame is drawn inside the product's real chrome, captured from Slate's /me/my-availability page on a local development server, signed in as an admin, 2026-10-27; main emptied (it is the slot) and the header reduced to a coordinator's: the Pennie logo and a generic SC account button, with no photo and no section links, from the committed fixture the built page renders too. The build is done when it scores 100% against this file on both halves.

Requirements

  • 7.1 A coordinator's header shows the Pennie logo and the account menu only, with no section links, since a coordinator opens no staff page (AppHeader drops ME_TABS for roles that open no staff page).
  • 7.2 The page is titled Book a call and holds a customer card above and a booking card below, and the booking card has no heading.
  • 7.3 The Salesforce Lead field looks the customer up as soon as it holds a whole Lead ID or Lead link, the Find customer button does the same, and the page carries no sample line.
  • 7.4 A Lead reference that is not a Lead ID or link, or one Salesforce does not hold, is refused under the field in plain words.
  • 7.5 The customer details are the name, the phone, and the time zone with the customer's clock now ('It's 10:40am for Jordan right now.'), with no Team row.
  • 7.6 The customer's time zone is named once on the page, in the customer details; every other time is the customer's own with no zone, except in the confirmation dialog.
  • 7.7 When the customer already has a call, the WarningNotice (rose, alert icon) names it and holds the checkbox 'I told {first} their {day} call will move'.
  • 7.8 Until that box is checked the whole booking card is locked, every control disabled and the hours line saying 'Check the box above after you tell {first}.', and unchecking locks it again keeping what was entered.
  • 7.9 A press or key on the locked card shows 'Check the box above first' with an up arrow over the card until the box is checked, says why in a polite live region, and pressing the message moves focus to the checkbox; motion is dropped under reduced motion.
  • 7.10 The booking card is laid out whole from the start (Day, the hours line, Time, the reason for a move, the confirm), every field the same gap apart, with no reserved empty space.
  • 7.11 The day is one row of the booker's day buttons from today through the last day Slate offers, counted as withinHorizon counts open days; a day in that span with no bookable time shows Closed and cannot be pressed.
  • 7.12 The hours line gives the chosen day's first and last bookable start times.
  • 7.13 Time is the booker's slot buttons grouped Morning and Afternoon, offering only bookable start times: inside Pennie's hours, inside the customer's 8am to 8pm calling window, on the quarter hour, and after now.
  • 7.14 A move asks Why the call is moving in SelectField, starting at Choose a reason, with the product's reschedule reasons, and Other opens a What happened box that must hold text.
  • 7.15 A refused main button is drawn in --bg-alt with a --fg-muted label and a not-allowed cursor, never Sky, with a note under it saying what is missing; this goes into the shared Button.
  • 7.16 The confirm button opens a confirmation dialog, a native modal, showing the customer's name, the day, the time and the time zone written out, and for a move the call it replaces; only its main button places the booking.
  • 7.17 The dialog's Go back and Escape close it and return focus to the confirm button, focus stays inside it while open, and while placing it shows the working state and ignores presses.
  • 7.18 Once placed, the page shows only the done state (Booked or Moved, the day and time, who the call is with, Booked by you or Moved by you) and one action, Book another call, which clears the page; Marcia is named only there.
  • 7.19 A booking is placed once: a repeated or reloaded submission is a replay by its submission id, and a customer found again shows the call just placed as their existing call.
  • 7.20 All page copy is short, plain English for a reader whose first language may not be English, and says Check, never Tick.
  • 7.21 The page is maintained in both the light and the dark theme.

Decisions

  1. D1 The layout is the customer card above and the booking card below, under the title Book a call. Dan's pick in round 2, 'Customer above, booking below': what was found reads apart from what is being chosen. Requirement 7.2. Checked on f-locked, f-sam-empty: main's h1 reads 'Book a call' and the heading 'Customer' comes before the group labelled Day.
  2. D2 The booking card has no heading. Settled with Dan in round 3: its fields, Day and Time, say what it holds, and a heading over them either disagreed with them ('New time') or repeated them. Requirement 7.2. Checked on f-unlocked, f-sam-empty: the only heading under the page title, outside any dialog, is 'Customer'.
  3. D3 An existing call is flagged by a warning notice with a bold line naming the customer, the call's day, time and advisor, and the checkbox 'I told Jordan their Tuesday call will move'. Dan's pick in round 3, 'Notice with a checkbox': making the coordinator confirm the customer was told is worth one more step on every move. Requirement 7.7. Checked on f-locked, f-pressed, f-unlocked: a status region opening 'Jordan already has a call' holds a checkbox whose label reads 'I told Jordan their Tuesday call will move'.
  4. D4 The notice wears WarningNotice's paint: a rose border and alert icon on a faint rose wash. Rose is the product's warning colour as well as its error one, settled with Dan on 2026-10-08; DESIGN.md keeps yellow for the impersonation marker alone. Requirement 7.7. Checked on f-locked, d-locked: the notice's border colour equals its alert icon's colour.
  5. D5 The move reason starts empty, reading 'Choose a reason'. Settled with Dan in round 3, so a reason is a choice the coordinator makes and never a default nobody chose. Requirement 7.14. Checked on f-locked, f-unlocked: the combobox labelled 'Why the call is moving' reads 'Choose a reason'.
  6. D6 The customer's time zone is named once, in the customer details; no other time on the page carries a zone, apart from the confirmation dialog. Dan's pick in round 4, 'Once, in the customer details': every fact appears once, and the coordinator reads the times as the customer's own. Requirement 7.6. Checked on f-locked, f-unlocked, f-done-move: outside the dialog, 'Arizona (Phoenix)' appears once while the customer is shown and not at all on the done page, and no other zone name appears.
  7. D7 The customer details are the name, the phone and the time zone; there is no Team row. Dan, round 5: coordinators never act on the team. Requirement 7.5. Checked on f-locked, f-sam-empty: the customer details list exactly two terms, Phone and Time zone.
  8. D8 Under the time zone, one line gives the customer's clock now: 'It's 10:40am for Jordan right now.'. Dan chose it in round 8 for coordinators who may be 'not smart, not technically literate, not motivated to do their job well because of low pay': the hour where the customer is, without working it out. Requirement 7.5. Checked on f-locked, f-sam-empty: the Time zone value includes 'It's 10:40am for Jordan right now.' for Jordan and 'It's 9:40am for Sam right now.' for Sam.
  9. D9 The whole booking card shows before a day is chosen, laid out as it is once one is: Day, the hours line holding 'Choose a day to see when calls can start.', Time unusable, and the confirm. Dan, round 5: nothing appears or disappears as the coordinator goes; things only fill in. Requirement 7.10. Checked on f-sam-empty: before a day, the card holds the Day group, the line 'Choose a day to see when calls can start.', the Time group and a disabled 'Book the call' button.
  10. D10 The checkbox gates the whole booking card: until it is checked every control is disabled and the hours line says 'Check the box above after you tell Jordan.'; unchecking locks it again and keeps what was entered. Dan, round 6: 'this checkbox does nothing. I should have to click it to do anything else on the page.'. Requirement 7.8. Checked on f-locked, f-pressed, d-locked: every day button, the reason and the confirm are disabled, and the page says 'Check the box above after you tell Jordan.'.
  11. D11 A press or key on the locked card puts 'Check the box above first', with an up arrow, over the card's fields until the box is checked; pressing it moves focus to the checkbox, and a polite live region says why. Dan, round 7: 'When I try to click the date or something without the checkbox, something needs to draw my attention to it.' He picked option 3, 'A pointer on the card'. Requirement 7.9. Checked on f-pressed: after a press on the locked card, a button reads 'Check the box above first'.
  12. D12 The day is one row of the booker's day buttons, from today through the last day Slate offers: Today, Tomorrow, Wed 18, Thu 19 in the fixture. Dan's pick in round 8, 'Two rows of day buttons', corrected in round 9: 'I thought we were only booking what slate shows? so like today slate would show 4 days out.' The span is counted as withinHorizon counts open days. Requirement 7.11. Checked on f-unlocked, f-sam-empty: the Day group's buttons are named 'Monday, November 16, Today', 'Tuesday, November 17', 'Wednesday, November 18' and 'Thursday, November 19', and no more.
  13. D13 A day inside the offered span with no bookable time shows Closed and cannot be pressed; a day whose hours are over does not count toward the span. Round 9, matching withinHorizon (src/lib/booking/placed-booking.ts): a day counts only while Pennie still has hours on it. Requirement 7.11. Checked on f-today-closed: on Monday evening the first day button is disabled and reads Closed, and the last is Friday, November 20.
  14. D14 The hours line gives the chosen day's first and last bookable start times. Sam's coordinator playbook (#4205): the coordinator reads the bookable hours out when a requested time is outside them. Requirement 7.12. Checked on f-unlocked: with Thursday chosen the page says 'On Thursday, November 19, calls can start from 8:00am to 5:30pm.'.
  15. D15 Only bookable start times can be chosen: inside Pennie's hours, inside the customer's 8am to 8pm calling window, on the quarter hour, and after now. Dan, round 5: 'don't let me even choose an invalid time'. Requirement 7.13. Checked on f-unlocked: the Time group's buttons are 8:00am to 5:30pm on the quarter hour, 39 of them, and nothing else.
  16. D16 Times are the booker's slot buttons, grouped Morning and Afternoon. Dan's pick in round 5, 'Time buttons, morning and afternoon': every bookable time at once, as the customer's own booker shows them. Requirement 7.13. Checked on f-unlocked: the Time group holds two groups headed Morning and Afternoon, the first all am times and the second all pm.
  17. D17 Nothing names Marcia before the call is booked; the line counting calls waiting with her is gone. Dan, round 6: remove the Marcia line and its count; the done state still says who the call is with. Requirement 7.18. Checked on f-unlocked, f-sam-empty: no text on the page names Marcia.
  18. D18 When the reason is Other, a 'What happened' box opens under the select, and the confirm stays refused until it holds text; another reason hides it and keeps the text. Dan, round 6; validateReason (src/lib/booking/core.ts) refuses Other without a detail. Requirement 7.14. Checked on f-other, d-other: a text box labelled 'What happened' is on the page and the note under the confirm reads 'Write what happened.'.
  19. D19 A refused main button is never Sky: it is the neutral --bg-alt fill with a --fg-muted label and a not-allowed cursor, with the note under it saying what is missing. Dan, round 7: 'It shouldn't be blue if I still have to do something.' He decided this applies to the shared Button everywhere. Requirement 7.15. Checked on f-other, d-other: the confirm button is disabled and its background colour differs from the chosen day button's.
  20. D20 All copy is short, plain English for a reader whose first language may not be English, with common words and no idioms; the page says Check, never Tick. Dan, round 8, of the coordinators: 'not smart, not technically literate, not motivated to do their job well because of low pay'. Requirement 7.20. Checked on f-locked, f-pressed, f-other, f-done-move: no text on the page contains the word Tick.
  21. D21 There is no 'Try a sample' line on the page; the Find customer button stays beside the Lead field. Dan, round 8: the sample IDs belong to the mock's readout, not the product. Requirement 7.3. Checked on f-locked, f-sam-empty, f-refusal: the page has no 'Try a sample' text and has a 'Find customer' button.
  22. D22 Pasting or typing a whole Lead ID or Lead link looks the customer up at once, with no button press. Dan, round 8: find on paste. Requirement 7.3. Proven by the coordinator page's component test under Requirement 7.3, which pastes an ID and asserts the lookup ran with no press.
  23. D23 The confirm button opens a confirmation dialog that reads the call back: the customer's name, the day and date, the time and the time zone written out, and for a move the call it replaces. Dan, round 10: 'I want a confirmation modal with the date/time/timezone before the booking goes through.' The zone may be said here, as this is the moment it is read back. Requirement 7.16. Checked on f-dialog-move, d-dialog-move: a modal dialog opens 'Move Jordan Rivera's call?' and says 'Thursday, November 19 at 2:00pm, Arizona time (Phoenix)' and 'Moves Jordan's Tuesday, November 17 at 11:00am call'.
  24. D24 The dialog's buttons are Go back (secondary) and Book the call or Move the call (the main button), and only the main button places the booking. Dan, round 10. Requirement 7.16. Checked on f-dialog-move, f-sam-dialog: the dialog's buttons are exactly 'Go back' then 'Move the call' for a move or 'Book the call' for a new booking.
  25. D25 For a new booking the dialog asks 'Book this call for Sam Okafor?' and says the day, time and zone, with no line about a call moving. Dan, round 10. Requirement 7.16. Checked on f-sam-dialog: the dialog opens 'Book this call for Sam Okafor?', says 'Tuesday, November 17 at 10:00am, Pacific time (Los Angeles)', and says nothing about a move.
  26. D26 Go back and Escape close the dialog with focus returned to the confirm button, focus stays inside it while it is open, and while placing it shows the working state and ignores presses. Dan, round 10. Requirement 7.17. Proven by the coordinator page's component test under Requirement 7.17, which opens the dialog, presses Escape and Go back, and presses the main button twice while placing.

Dan, round 8: 'No double booking'; every field is gone, so nothing can be placed twice. Requirement 7.18. The product books the placeholder (Marcia) when nobody is free; the done state is the one place she is named (round 6). Requirement 7.18. Checked on f-sam-marcia: the page reads exactly 'Book a call Booked Tuesday, November 17 at 4:00pm With Marcia, Pennie's AI agent. No advisor was free at this time, so Marcia will text Sam.   30. D30 A booking is placed once: a repeated or reloaded submission is a replay by its submission id, and a customer found again shows the call just placed as their existing call.   32. D32 A coordinator's header shows the Pennie logo and the account menu only, with no section links. Dan, round 9: 'this isn't what they'd see.' Requirement 7.1. Held by the picture half.
  33. D33 Every field in the booking card is the same gap apart, Jordan's and Sam's alike, with no reserved or empty space anywhere. Dan, round 6, on the gap the Marcia line had reserved between Time and the reason. Requirement 7.10. Held by the picture half.

Dan, 2026-10-26: "don't worry about scripting anything, we'll give them a script". Round 4's option of putting the zone into sentences read aloud was not chosen. No code: Copy rule: nothing to build beyond the copy the requirements already fix. Held by the picture half.

Idioms of the medium, to translate rather than copy

  • The checkbox's tick, the dialog's scrim and the slot dots are drawn from the product's own classes; their exact pixels follow the browser.
  • The mock's lookups and placements are timers standing in for the server; the working states they show are the product's.
  • The fixture's clock is fixed (Monday, November 16, 12:40pm in New York) so every frame draws the same days and times.
  • The locked card's catcher is a transparent button over the card, a web idiom for taking a press on disabled controls; the build may use any means that takes the press and key.
