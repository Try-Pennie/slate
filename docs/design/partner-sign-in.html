Partner coordinator sign in

For the call center partner's scheduling coordinators, who sign in with their work Microsoft account. Settled in one round on 2026-10-29, approved by Dan: partners get a sign in page of their own, and the staff login stays as it is.

The settled design. Every frame is drawn inside the product's real chrome, captured from https://slate.trypennie.app/login, signed out, captured 2026-10-29, from the committed fixture the built page renders too. The build is done when it scores 100% against this file on both halves.

Requirements

  • 4379.4 A partner coordinator signs in with their work Microsoft account from a sign in page of their own, and the staff login stays Google only.

Decisions

  1. D1 The partner sign in page offers one way in, Continue with Microsoft, and no Google button. Partners cannot use Google and staff cannot use Microsoft, so each sees only the way in they can use. Requirement 4379.4. Checked on f1: the sign in card has exactly one button, and it reads Continue with Microsoft.
  2. D2 The card is headed Sign in to book calls, with the line Sign in with your work Microsoft account to book calls for Pennie's customers. It names what the partner is there to do, in their words. Requirement 4379.4. Checked on f1: the card's heading reads Sign in to book calls and its line reads Sign in with your work Microsoft account to book calls for Pennie's customers.
  3. D3 The staff login page is unchanged. Staff keep the page they know, with no Microsoft option. No code: Nothing on /login changes. Proven by src/app/login/page.test.tsx.

Exceptions Dan approved

None. A deliberate difference from this design is a new design round, or an exception Dan approves in his own words, recorded here.
